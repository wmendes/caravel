Caravel

For Stellar apps that need their own rules.

Run your app as a lane: your block time, fees, members and validators, in one file. Open source.

lane.tomlexample
[node]
block_time_ms = 500      # every 0.5 s
[access]
mode = "allowlist"       # members only
[[perps.markets]]
maker_fee_bps = 0        # your fees
[env.testnet]
threshold = 2            # your validators
token = "circle-usdc"    # settles in USDC
Your users' money stays on Stellar.
