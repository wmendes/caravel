First lane: Caravel Perps

At 5-second blocks, quotes go stale.

Our first lane is a trading venue, and trading needs speed. Stellar was built for payments, and it's excellent at them. Trading is different. A market maker's quote on a 5-second chain sits frozen while the price moves, and anyone who sees the real price can trade against it. So makers widen spreads or leave, and volume goes to faster chains.

How it works

Every dollar starts and ends on Stellar.

A lane is its own Stellar-based chain with its own validators and rules. It runs Soroban, so existing contracts, SDKs and wallets carry over. Stellar holds the money and checks the lane's work.

Top band: a Caravel lane Bottom band: Stellar mainnet, ~5s ledgers
  1. Stellar → lane

    Deposit

    Lock USDC in the settlement contract on Stellar. The same amount appears on the lane.

  2. On the lane

    Trade at 1s

    Orders match on a Soroban order book in every 1-second block.

  3. Lane → Stellar

    Checkpoint

    Every few blocks, the validators sign the lane's new state and it's posted to Stellar, where a contract verifies it.

  4. On Stellar

    Withdraw

    USDC is released back to your Stellar wallet, and only against a verified checkpoint.

Trust model

Fast on the lane. Final on Stellar.

A trade confirms in about a second. It becomes final once Stellar has verified the checkpoint that contains it.

~1s

Soft confirmation

The lane's validators confirm your trade in the next block. Between checkpoints you're trusting them, the same trade-off every rollup makes.

Checkpoint + ~5s

Hard settlement

Once the settlement contract verifies the checkpoint and a Stellar ledger closes, the trade is final. You're trusting Stellar and the math.

Planned

If the lane stops

Your USDC never leaves Stellar. The exit path lets you withdraw against the last verified checkpoint.

Why now

Verifying a lane on Stellar just got cheap.

Checking another chain's work inside a Stellar contract used to be impractical. Four protocol upgrades and one optimization changed that.

  1. Dec 2024

    Protocol 22

    BLS12-381 host functions, so validators can aggregate signatures (CAP-59).

  2. Jan 2026

    Protocol 25

    BN254 and Poseidon, the building blocks of most ZK proofs (CAP-74 and CAP-75).

  3. May 2026

    Protocol 26

    Faster BN254 math for proof checks (CAP-80).

  4. Jun 2026

    Circle CCTP

    Native USDC moves between Stellar and other chains.

  5. Aug 2026

    Nethermind

    Noir UltraHonk proof checks on Soroban made 64% cheaper.

  6. Oct 2026

    Caravel

    Custom lanes that checkpoint to Stellar.

Roadmap

From trusted validators to trustless lanes.

Each step asks users to trust less.

Now

Signed checkpoints

Three validators run by the team. Stellar checks their aggregated BLS signature on every checkpoint.

Next · Q4 2026

Validity proofs

A ZK proof replaces the signature check. The exit path and a public testnet ship with it.

Then

Bonded validators

Anyone can run a validator. Rewards are a share of fees in USDC, vesting and slashable.

Later

Lane launch kit

Pick block time, gas token, fees and validators, and deploy a lane settled on Stellar.
