# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Stellar developers and hackathon teams first. They come to see what a Caravel lane can do, and they trade to find out. Second, crypto-native traders who know Hyperliquid and dYdX and will judge us against them. Everyone is on testnet with test USDC, but they expect a real exchange. They connect a Stellar wallet, deposit, place orders and watch positions, and some want to understand why a fill is "soft" until a checkpoint settles it on Stellar.

## Product Purpose

Caravel Perps is a perpetual futures exchange on a Caravel lane: an order book with an oracle mark price, settled to Stellar in Circle's testnet USDC. The app has to work like a professional perps terminal, and it has to show what a lane is while it does. Every number says where it stands: soft on the lane, or settled on Stellar. Success means a developer trades in under a minute, understands soft versus settled without reading docs, and leaves thinking "this is a real exchange, on Stellar".

## Brand Personality

Precise, fast, honest. Numbers first, no noise. The app never overstates: testnet only, not audited, and "soft" until Stellar has accepted the checkpoint. The tone is plain and exact, like a good trading terminal's labels, never hype.

## Anti-references

- A toy or hackathon demo: placeholder panels, debug text, unfinished states.
- A generic SaaS dashboard: cards everywhere, big rounded panels, hero metrics.
- CEX clutter: promo banners, upsells, a wall of widgets (Binance-style).
- A casino or meme DEX: neon gradients, confetti, leaderboards, "degen" styling.

## Design Principles

1. **The terminal disappears into the trade.** Familiar perps layout and vocabulary (chart, book, ticket, positions); no reinvented affordances.
2. **Soft vs settled, always visible.** Lane state and Stellar state are distinct everywhere they appear, in color and in words.
3. **Show what is true, nothing else.** No fabricated metrics, no implied guarantees; empty and degraded states say exactly what is missing.
4. **Teach in place.** A newcomer learns the lane (deposit, trading key, checkpoints, escape) from the interface itself, at the moment it matters.
5. **Density with order.** Many numbers on one screen, aligned and tabular, with a clear hierarchy of what to look at first.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and full keyboard use. Color is never the only signal: green and red always come with a sign, arrow or label (long/short, buy/sell, +/−). Reduced motion respected. Numbers use tabular figures so columns don't jitter as prices update.
